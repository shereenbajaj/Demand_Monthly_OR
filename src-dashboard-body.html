How the two pools relate.
  • Online and offline are separate, not a split of one total. total_donation and total_orders count online only; offline_amount and offline_count sit alongside them and are added to nothing. Money raised in a month is online + offline — for all categories in Aug 26 that is ₹6.36 Cr + ₹1.10 Cr = ₹7.46 Cr. That combined figure is what the header and the four category tiles show; every other figure outside the Offline payments section is online.
  • Offline is almost entirely Personal medical. It runs about 21% of that category's money raised, against 0.1–0.3% for the other three. A near-zero offline figure elsewhere is the real picture, not missing data.
  • Unique donors and unique visitors do not add across categories. Anyone who gave to two categories is counted in both, so the All-categories figures for those two are upper bounds. Per-category figures are exact.
